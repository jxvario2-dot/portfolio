My world
outside STEM.

I am Joshua Vario. Most people that know me know me by my deep interest in STEM, however, there is more to me than that. My interest in the universe does run deep, but I also have interest elsewhere: I enjoy writing poetry, photographing our beautiful world, and thinking deeply about topics such as morality and value.

01 / ABOUT

A few things that I enjoy.

This site is a collection of the things I spend my time thinking about, creating, and photographing.

I enjoy exploring ideas from both analytical and creative perspectives. Mathematics and physics give me ways to understand the structure of the universe, while poetry and photography let me explore experiences that are harder to express with equations.


This website is a record of some of those interests—not a résumé, but a place to show what I make and what I find worth exploring.

"Doors"

Original poem

My world’s lights burnt out My world’s lock got stuck My world flooded The air escaped my lungs I grabbed for the holds that hid in the shadows But the desperation pulled me under Many walked past the door From the outside, it was just any other door But inside, the salt water ate away at my body and mind

I heard a knock And a spark returned I swam down to the door and struggled with the lock Minutes went by Hours went by Was anyone even at the door anymore? Days went by I struggled with the lock And the floodgates opened Nobody was there But I was glad I only needed myself I wouldn’t depend on someone else to fix my world I relit my world It was brighter than ever before I removed the once broken lock and propped the door open The floors were still wet But just damp And I didn’t want them to dry I finally rested I heard a knock But the knock wasn’t what awoke me It was the light that came with it A light so bright it outshone my own A light so warm it felt like a blanket wrapped around my heart

"Change"

Original poem

Nearby rustling woke me from my sleep When my eyes opened, I saw mother But her eyes were concerned She told me our foraging spot disappeared It was taken by beasts, she said Beasts led by humans We left in search of a new foraging spot The moon was overhead

We reached a barren strip We had crossed multiple already But as mother crossed, I bleated out A beast was running at her She stood still, looking into the beast’s sun-eyes Once it pounced on her, she flew The beast lost sight of her and moved on I cautiously stepped onto the barren strip and walked to her She was asleep I bleated to try to wake her, but she remained asleep I laid down with her, waiting for her to wake
